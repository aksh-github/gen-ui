## Plain Language Scoring

This assessment tool can be used to assess everyday content, according to industry-standard plain language principles as well as Acme Inc brand adherence. As human bias and interpretation can lead to inconsistencies in assessing content, this tool enables content creators to be sure they are adhering to best practices.

It provides scoring against the metrics below:

- Long Sentence Score:
- Passive Voice Score
- Jargon Detection Score
- Acme Inc Brand Voice Score: alignment with Acme Inc brand character and tone
- English Readability (Flesch reading ease)
- French Readability (LIX readability)
- Grade Level (Flesch-kincaid grade level)

## Marketing Email Classification

This tool is trained on the Canadian Email Classification Framework and content guidelines, to support teams in developing and deploying Marketing emails in Canada.

Requirement changes from email platforms like Google and Yahoo resulted in the need to update our email practices at Acme Inc, otherwise we risked emails being flagged as spam or not delivering.

The backend prompt template is fed with information about:

- Canadian email classification framework: distinguishing commercial emails (cross-sell and up-sell) from service/transactional emails
- Service Best Practices at Acme Inc
- Cross-sell & Up-sell Best Practices at Acme Inc
- Legal background guidelines
